Forward,

Design

Same shape.
New finish.

The 5s kept the iPhone 5's anodized aluminum unibody and 4-inch Retina display, but introduced two finishes that would define Apple's palette for years: Space Gray and Gold. Keep scrolling and watch it come apart, piece by piece.

  • Aluminum unibody in three finishes
  • 4-inch Retina display, 1136 × 640 at 326 ppi
  • 112 grams — slimmer and lighter than the 5

Camera

One lens.
Two-tone flash.

The 5s introduced True Tone flash — two LEDs, warm and cool, blended for over 1,000 combinations to match ambient light. Paired with larger 1.5µm pixels for real gains in low light.

8MP iSight camera
120fps Slo-mo video

Performance

A7. The chip
that changed phones.

The first 64-bit chip in any smartphone — twice the registers, twice the general-purpose registers, up to 2x the performance of the A6. The M7 motion coprocessor, sitting alongside it, quietly launched an entire fitness-tracking industry.

  • 64-bit architecture — a first for phones
  • M7 motion coprocessor for always-on sensor data
  • Up to 2x CPU & graphics vs. A6

Touch ID

Press.
You're in.

A capacitive fingerprint sensor built into the Home button, covered in sapphire crystal. It read sub-epidermal skin layers at 500 ppi — and it's the reason unlocking your phone with a finger feels completely unremarkable today.
